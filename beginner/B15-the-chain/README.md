B15 - THE CHAIN

Points: 5  
Category: Beginner / Multi-Stage Puzzle  

Challenge Overview  
Real-world cybersecurity investigations frequently require chaining multiple pieces of evidence together. Solving one piece of data yields a clue or path pointing toward the next component of the investigation. In this challenge, you will start with a binary file, decode its pointer, and trace the chain to the final flag.

Participant Question  
One clue leads to another. Don't stop when you find something that looks like an answer. Follow the chain.

Clue  
Decode the binary numbers in `start.bin` to discover the name of the next clue file. Open that file and follow its instructions to the final flag.

Challenge Files  
- `challenge/start.bin`
- `challenge/clue.txt`
- `challenge/final_flag.txt`

Flag Format  
`flag{...}`
