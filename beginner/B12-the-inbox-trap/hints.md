# Hints: B12 - THE INBOX TRAP

### Hint 1
The inbox dump contains dozens of diverse email threads including newsletters, notifications, automated alerts, and phishing decoys.

### Hint 2
Attackers frequently craft urgent security or identity verification warnings impersonating major social or technology platforms to prompt urgent clicks.

### Hint 3
Locate the deceptive account verification lure targeting the user and examine the unique campaign tracking identifier or reference string included in the message footer.
