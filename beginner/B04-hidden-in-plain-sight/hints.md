# Hints: B04 - HIDDEN IN PLAIN SIGHT

### Hint 1
When you browse or list the contents of a directory, default operating system behavior does not necessarily present the entire contents of a folder.

### Hint 2
Operating systems frequently categorize certain entries to remain invisible during routine navigation to avoid clutter or accidental modification.

### Hint 3
Review the manual pages or command-line options of standard directory listing tools to display all files and folders without omission.
